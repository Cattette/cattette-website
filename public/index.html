Home

Welcome to my personal website. My name is cattette, and I am a land surveyor living in Sweden.

This website serves as my personal weblog, where I share my artwork and other things that interest me. It replaces my previous presence on Reddit, Twitter and DeviantArt, as I have grown increasingly cynical about the direction and overall state of those (and many other!) platforms over the years.

About this site

As someone with 0 experience in webdevelopment the production of this site has gone through several stages. Early attempts at typing the entire thing in individual html files proved tedious and attempts at automating the process with bash scripts, Pandoc, and Python also grew cumbersome.

I am currently building this site with Hugo, an open-source static site generator. A static site generator produces static html files from markdown files, html templates, and data files. This makes them ideal for small sites such as blogs or documentation sites which only need to update their information once in a while and less suitable for social networks or large storefronts.
